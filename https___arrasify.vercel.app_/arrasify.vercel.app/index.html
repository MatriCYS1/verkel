Getting Started - Rules

What Isn't Allowed

Spamming

Pretty obvious. If multiple users deem you to be spamming, either through messages or commands, stop immediately. If continued, you will receive punishment.

Advertising

Anything promotional that is irrelevent to the conversation currently being held should not be promoted. This rule also oftenly interlaces into spam, and is treated as such.

Language

Most of us speak English, but other languages are allowed. However, refrain from using extreme slurs and hateful words. One or two are fine, but if you send too many, you will likely be punished. This rule ties into hate & prejudice.

This rule is lenient, so read the audience before you post.

Media

Refrain from posting graphic imagery, implied innuendos, NSFW content, and anything that you think people would likely get uncomfortable with. Punishment is usually immediate, so think before you post.

Hate & Prejudice

Any kind of prejudice or hate towards anyone, especially catered to specific groups, will usually be warned. If continued, expect exponential punishment.

This rule includes, but is not limited to racism, homophobia, transphobia, anti-religion, and health conditions. Make sure your message comes across the right way before posting.

Evasion

Evading punishment, whether it be using a guest account, a vpn, an alternate account, a role, or any other number of methods will result in exponential severe punishment.

VPNs are allowed, but only if used not for the purpose of punishment evasion.

Direct Messages

Don't send anything inappropriate in DMs. Yes, it's an unmonitored area, but if the user is uncomfortable, depending on what is said, you can and will probably get punished.

Shady People

Anyone already confirmed to be suspicious, proven to broken any aforementioned rules, or done something not appropriate, will be removed without hesitation.

Any attempts at trying to evade punishments will result in exponentially worse punishment.

Punishments

Block

You will be unable to send direct messages to specific users. The users that block you will also have the option to hide your global messages. This is decided by accounts of Arrasify, and not explicitly chosen by admins.

Mute

You won't be able to send messages for a duration of time. Until that time is over, you cannot communicate to anyone, including DMs. You can be muted in either text, uploads, or both. Mutes are given to people who advertise, spam, or send inappropriate language.

Temp-Ban

You will temporarily be unable to access your account, until the specified duration has passed. You can still see the chat as a guest, but cannot interact with anyone. This is given to people if rules are broken continuously.

Suspension

You will be unable to access your account for an undisclosed amount of time. This is used when admins aren't sure how long you should wait to be forgiven.

Termination

Your account will be completely deleted from the database. Your token will no longer function, and you will need to ask for a new one. This is reserved for extreme rule infraction, often accompanied by shunning or blacklisting.

Shunning

You will not be able to access the site, or any utilities, until the specified duration has passed. All routes and services will be blocked. This is reserved for extreme rule infraction.

Blacklist

You are permanently unable to use our site or our services. This is used if you evade punishments too much, have a bad history of chat infraction, or done other activities that prompt this action.

Moderation Tips for Admins

Moderation from admin-to-admin varies. Because of this, some users may think admins are biased for letting some users get away with breaking the rules. We have no proper standard to follow aside the rules and the appropriate punishments, so if you're accused of being biased, try see what other admins are doing to get a better idea on what should be done.

Inaccurate punishments given to users are fine if they are mistakes, but if it's a continous or conscious choice, you will be demoted. Not everyone is meant to be an admin, and that may not necessarily be your fault, so please don't take it personally. We've seen a few prior admins in old verify tend to have this reaction.

Also, don't feel pressured to be chronically online 24/7. This community might be expanding, but you do not have to sacrifice your own time stressing about the state of this service. It is not a job, and being an admin should be purely based on genuinely wanting to help out, and not for status or obligation.

Getting Started - Admins

This section may contain out-of-date information.

Different Admin Types

Helper

Synopsis

Helpers are the first type of administrator, or moderator. Their purpose is to help lightly moderate the chat, either by muting people who break the basic rules, or directing users to other admins who can help.

Permissions

Mute

Can mute guests and users, for a specific duration of time. The range can be from one minute to four hours.

Underseer

Synopsis

Underseers can do everything helpers can, and also create, edit, and delete accounts. They can promote and demote specific users to and from helper, and can dish out temp-bans.

Permissions

Mute

Mute time range is increased to eight hours.

Temp-Ban

Able to temporarily ban access from accounts of users.

Account Manipulation

Can create, edit, and delete entries from the database of accounts. Can only access the specific codes they themselves added, and as such can also only modify or delete those codes.

Can promote and demote users to and from helpers.

Overseer

Synopsis

Overseers can do everything underseers do, and can modify all accounts without restriction, except for roles higher above itself. Promotion permissions are still the same.

Permissions

Mute

Mute time range is increased to twelve hours.

Slowmode

Able to add a slowmode, in between zero seconds to one minute.

Suspension

Can permanently stop users from logging into accounts, for an undisclosed time.

Account Manipulation

Can edit every account except for authentication higher than itself.

Admin

Synopsis

(Not to be confused with all authenticated accounts also called 'admins').

Admins can do everything overseers do, and can change dynamically-configured settings of the server, save entire chat channel history by downloading it, make announcements in the inbox, and restart the server at will.

Permissions

Mute

Mute time range increased to two days.

Slowmode

Increase of maximum slowmode interval to an hour.

Shunning

Able to temporarily make accounts unable to access the site entirely. Has a range of one day to seven days.

Account Manipulation

Able to promote users to overseers.

Dynamics

Can change dynamic statistics about the server, such as message recall, being able to download the current chat history since server uptime, and a few other things.

Mail Privileges

Can send news posts regarding updates to all users, via the inbox.

Restart

Can restart the server, forcing users to re-authenticate. Has a cooldown of five minutes.

Developer

Synopsis

Can do everything, without restriction, domain, cooldown, or specific circumstances.

Permissions

Could probably delete Arrasify entirely if they wanted.
